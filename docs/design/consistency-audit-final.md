contradicted nowhere; and the nine §120 tests have one owner per level plus a by-name pin. The only
substantive open question left anywhere is **BT-2** — whether `certificates` and `student_id_cards` are
append-only — and it changes two `deleted_at` columns on non-financial tables, not a money path.
